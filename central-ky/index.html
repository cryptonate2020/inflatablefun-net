Bounce House Rentals in Central Kentucky

Delivery and setup for backyard birthdays, school events, and community parties across the Bluegrass.

Party Central of Kentucky rents bounce houses, wet/dry slides, combos, and obstacle courses throughout Central Kentucky. For 30+ years we have helped families, schools, churches, and companies turn ordinary weekends into the party everyone remembers — from a quiet Lexington backyard to a crowded field day farther out on the belt.

Most requests start the same way: kids need somewhere safe to bounce while adults grill and catch up. A classic open bounce or castle fits smaller yards; when you have more lawn (and more energy to burn), a bounce-and-slide combo or a wet/dry slide turns Saturday into an all-day affair. Tell us ages, guest count, and roughly how much space you have — we steer you toward units that match the crowd instead of overselling the tallest slide on the lot.

Delivery and setup are part of how we work. Our crew brings the inflatable, stakes or weights it for your surface, inflates it, and walks you through the basics before the kids pile in. When the rental window ends we return for takedown so you are not left deflating a giant castle after cake. Power needs are usually a standard outdoor outlet on a dedicated circuit; we confirm details when we quote so there are fewer surprises on party day.

Kentucky afternoons can shift from calm to windy in a hurry. If lightning, high wind, or heavy rain makes conditions unsafe, call us as early as you can and we will work with you to reschedule. We would rather move the date than run a unit when it is not safe. We carry insurance and set up with care so schools, HOAs, and private hosts can feel confident about the equipment on site.

Central Kentucky FAQ

Which towns do you deliver to?

We deliver and set up across Central Kentucky, including Lexington, Nicholasville, Versailles, Georgetown, Paris, Lawrenceburg, Richmond, and nearby communities. Put your event address on the quote form and we will confirm coverage for your date.

How much yard space do I need?

Each unit lists dimensions on its product page. Plan for the footprint plus clearance on the sides and enough height for taller slides. Soft, level ground works best; we will talk through driveways, decks, and HOA rules when we quote.

What happens if the weather turns bad?

Safety comes first. If wind, lightning, or heavy rain makes conditions unsafe, call us early and we will help you reschedule. We would rather move the party than operate in unsafe weather.

Can I rent for a school or church event?

Absolutely. Many of our jobs are school field days, church picnics, and community events. Tell us if you need a Certificate of Insurance when you request a quote.
