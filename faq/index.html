FAQ

Quick answers about booking, weather, delivery, and more.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekends and popular summer dates fill up fast. We recommend requesting a quote as soon as you have a date in mind — ideally a few weeks ahead for weekends, though we'll do our best with shorter notice when inventory allows.

How does the quote process work?

Submit a Request a Quote form with your date, location, and the items you're interested in. We'll review availability and get back to you with pricing and options. You can also call 859-887-0860 or email partycentralofkentucky@yahoo.com.

What if the weather looks bad?

Kentucky weather can turn quickly. If wind, lightning, or heavy rain makes conditions unsafe for your event, give us a call as early as possible — we're happy to work with you on rescheduling. Safety comes first, and we'd rather move your date than run a unit in unsafe conditions.

How much space and power do I need?

Each unit has different footprint and height requirements — check the product page for dimensions, and leave extra clearance around the unit. Most inflatables need a standard outdoor outlet on a dedicated circuit; we'll confirm power needs when we quote your order.

Do you deliver, or can I pick up?

We deliver and set up most inflatables across Central Kentucky. Some smaller games and concessions are available for customer pick-up — see Customer Pick-up Games for those options.

Can you provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) for schools or venues?

Yes. Many schools, churches, and venues require a COI. Let us know when you request a quote and we'll arrange the paperwork with the certificate holder details you provide.

Do you require a deposit?

Quotes are free and no deposit is due when you first inquire. Once your rental is approved and dates are locked in, we'll arrange a deposit to secure your reservation. Call or email if you have questions about payment timing.
