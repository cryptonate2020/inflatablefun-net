Nicholasville FAQ

Do you serve all of Jessamine County?

We regularly deliver in Nicholasville and nearby Jessamine County communities. Put your event address on the quote form and we will confirm coverage and timing for your date.

How far ahead should I book a weekend party?

Summer Saturdays go first. Request a quote as soon as you have a date — a few weeks ahead is ideal for weekends, though we will do our best with shorter notice when inventory allows.

Is setup included with delivery?

Yes for our inflatable rentals. We deliver, set up, and return for takedown. Some smaller games are customer pick-up — see Customer Pick-up Games if you want those options.

Can I book wet and dry options for the same day?

Many slides and combos can run wet or dry depending on weather and your preference. Tell us what you want when you quote, and we will note water access and safety guidelines for your yard.
