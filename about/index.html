About Us

Party Central of Kentucky — We Rent Fun!

Party Central of Kentucky has been helping families, schools, churches, and companies throw memorable events for 30+ years. From backyard birthdays to corporate picnics, we deliver bounce houses, water slides, combos, obstacle courses, games, and more across Lexington, Nicholasville, and surrounding Central Kentucky.

We take safety seriously. Our team sets up and takes down equipment with care, and we carry insurance so you can focus on the party. If wind, lightning, or heavy rain makes conditions unsafe, we'll work with you to reschedule — safety always comes first.
