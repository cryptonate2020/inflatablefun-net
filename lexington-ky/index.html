Most Lexington rentals start with a simple request: a safe place to bounce for the kids while adults grill and catch up. A classic castle or open bounce house fits smaller yards; when you have more lawn (and more energy to burn), a bounce-and-slide combo or a wet/dry slide turns an ordinary Saturday into the party everyone talks about. We help you match the unit to your space, age range, and whether you want dry play only or a water day when the forecast cooperates.

Delivery and setup are part of how we work. Our crew brings the inflatable, stakes or weights it for your surface, inflates it, and walks you through the basics before the kids pile in. Power needs are usually a standard outdoor outlet on a dedicated circuit — we confirm details when we quote so there are fewer surprises on party day.

We carry insurance and set up with care so schools, HOAs, and private hosts can feel confident about the equipment on site.

Lexington FAQ

Do you deliver bounce houses inside Lexington?

Yes. We deliver and set up across Lexington and surrounding Central Kentucky. Include your event address and date on the quote form so we can confirm availability for your neighborhood.

How much yard space do I need?

Each unit lists dimensions on its product page. Plan for the footprint plus clearance on the sides and enough height for taller slides. Soft, level ground works best; we will talk through driveways, decks, and HOA rules when we quote.

What happens if the weather turns bad?

Safety comes first. If wind, lightning, or heavy rain makes conditions unsafe, call us early and we will help you reschedule. We would rather move the party than operate in unsafe weather.

Can I rent for a school or church event in Lexington?

Absolutely. Many of our Lexington jobs are school field days, church picnics, and community events. Tell us if you need a Certificate of Insurance when you request a quote.
